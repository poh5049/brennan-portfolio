About

The work. The person.

I'm Brennan. Interior designer. Four years, close to seventy projects — residential and commercial. Every one designed with the same conviction: that a space should be so precisely right that it needs no explanation.

My design language draws from Japanese restraint — the discipline of removing everything that does not serve the space, and refining what remains until it is exactly what it needs to be. Layered with a luxury sensibility that comes from working across high-specification residential and commercial projects. The two inform each other. Commercial rigour makes residential design sharper. I bring both to every brief.

I am not the designer for everyone. I work with clients who understand that the difference between a well-executed renovation and a truly considered space is invisible until you've lived in both — and then impossible to ignore.

What I design

  • Premium residential — condo, landed, high-specification HDB
  • Commercial — F&B, boutique retail, luxury office
  • Full concept to completion, including material and finish specification
  • Site supervision — I am present, every time

The person

I recently became a father and moved into my own home — which changes how you think about every decision you make for someone else's family. I am drawn to commercial projects for the rigour they demand. And I have been recognised three consecutive years for SG Interior's top performance — something I mention not as a credential, but as evidence that the commitment behind the work is consistent.

My aspiration is straightforward: to be known, across Singapore and the region, as the designer whose spaces you commission when you want something that will still be exactly right in twenty years.

If that is what you are looking for — commission a conversation.
