About

Spaces with identity.

Brennan Phua is a Singapore-based senior interior designer at SG Interior Design, known for creating homes and commercial spaces that are both beautiful and practical. His work is guided by contrast — light and dark, bold and calm, open and cosy — resulting in interiors that feel personal, thoughtful, and never generic.

With a focus on materials, atmosphere, and everyday living, Brennan designs for clients who want more than a standard renovation. They want a space that feels like their own — a place to live, rest, host, and enjoy.
